# Guided Sessions — Calm and Morning

Five short guided sessions read aloud by Android text-to-speech at a calm pace: a morning intention, a stress reset, a grounding practice, a gratitude pause and a kindness practice. Format: DESIGN_PHASE2.md section 3. Each script line is one spoken line, and a trailing pause adds silence after it.

## Session — Morning intention
```json session
{"id": "morning_intention", "title": "Morning intention", "minutes": 3, "category": "MORNING",
 "description": "Breathe, notice how you feel, and choose one simple intention for the day.", "bestFor": "Starting the day with a clear, calm focus",
 "ambient": "focus_pad"}
```
Good morning. [pause 3]
Find a position that feels comfortable. Sit, stand, or lie down. Eyes open or closed. [pause 3]
This practice is for relaxation. If you feel unwell or distressed, please reach out to a doctor or another professional. [pause 4]
Take a slow breath in, and let it go. [pause 5]
Once more. In, and out. [pause 5]
Now notice how you feel this morning. [pause 4]
Rested, heavy, or somewhere in between. Whatever is here is welcome. [pause 7]
Let your shoulders drop. [pause 4]
Now think about the day ahead. Not the whole list, just its shape. [pause 5]
Ask yourself, how would I like to meet this day? [pause 6]
Perhaps with patience. Perhaps with energy, kindness, or focus. [pause 4]
Let one word or phrase come to you. Something simple and true. [pause 10]
This is your intention for today. Say it quietly to yourself. [pause 6]
Picture one moment today when it might help. A conversation, a task, or a walk. [pause 8]
You can return to it whenever you like. It is a guide, not a test. [pause 5]
Take one more slow breath in. [pause 3]
And out. [pause 4]
Feel the surface beneath you, and the sounds around you. [pause 4]
When you are ready, open your eyes. [pause 3]
Take a breath, and step into your day. [pause 4]

## Session — Two-minute stress reset
```json session
{"id": "stress_reset", "title": "Two-minute stress reset", "minutes": 3, "category": "CALM",
 "description": "Soften your body, take a few long exhales, name the feeling, and choose one small next step.", "bestFor": "A tense moment at work, at home, or on the move",
 "ambient": "pink"}
```
Pause here for a moment. Wherever you are, you can reset. [pause 4]
Stay seated, stand, or lean on a wall. Eyes open or closed. [pause 4]
First, let your shoulders drop, away from your ears. [pause 5]
Let your hands soften. Let your jaw loosen, and your tongue rest. [pause 6]
Now a long breath in, through your nose if that suits you. [pause 3]
And a slow, long breath out, as if you are cooling a hot cup of tea. [pause 7]
Again. In. [pause 3]
And out, a little longer. [pause 6]
One more. In, and a long, slow breath out. [pause 8]
Let your breathing return to its own pace. [pause 5]
Notice what you are feeling. A tight chest, a busy mind, or a restless body. [pause 5]
Give it a name. You might say quietly, this is stress, and it will pass. [pause 7]
You are not doing anything wrong. Stress is part of being human. [pause 6]
Now ask yourself, what is one small next step? [pause 6]
Not the whole problem. Just the next small thing. [pause 4]
Maybe sending one message, drinking some water, or standing up to stretch. [pause 6]
Choose one, and let it be small enough to feel easy. [pause 7]
Take one more breath, and feel your feet or your seat beneath you. [pause 4]
When you are ready, open your eyes, and take your small next step. [pause 4]

## Session — 5-4-3-2-1 grounding
```json session
{"id": "grounding_54321", "title": "5-4-3-2-1 grounding", "minutes": 3, "category": "CALM",
 "description": "Use your senses to come back to the present: five things you see, four you feel, three you hear, two you smell, one you taste.", "bestFor": "When your mind feels busy, scattered or overwhelmed",
 "ambient": null}
```
Let's begin by noticing that you are here. [pause 3]
Sit, stand, or lie down. Keep your eyes open or closed, whichever feels better. [pause 3]
We will use your senses, counting down from five to one. [pause 3]
Take a slow breath in, and out. [pause 4]
Five things you can see. Look around slowly, and quietly name them. [pause 12]
If you cannot see, or your eyes are closed, notice five sounds, or five places your body touches something. [pause 6]
Four things you can feel. The weight of your body, your clothes on your skin, the air on your face, and your hands, warm or cool. [pause 10]
Three things you can hear. Near, then far. A fan, traffic, birds, or the quiet itself. [pause 10]
Two things you can smell. Perhaps tea, soap, cooking, or fresh air. [pause 8]
If you cannot smell, or nothing is there, imagine two smells you love. Or take two slow breaths. [pause 6]
One thing you can taste. Notice your mouth, or take a sip of water. Or take one slow, full breath. [pause 8]
Now notice how you feel, compared with a few minutes ago. [pause 6]
Nothing needs to have changed. Noticing is enough. [pause 4]
Whenever things feel fast, you can come back to your senses like this. [pause 4]
When you are ready, take a breath, and open your eyes if they were closed. [pause 4]

## Session — Gratitude pause
```json session
{"id": "gratitude", "title": "Gratitude pause", "minutes": 4, "category": "CALM",
 "description": "Three breaths, three simple good things from your day, and one to savour and carry forward.", "bestFor": "Ending a busy day, or lifting a flat mood",
 "ambient": "rain"}
```
Welcome. Let's take a few minutes to notice what is good. [pause 4]
Settle into a position that feels easy. Eyes open, resting on something calm, or closed. [pause 4]
Let's begin with three breaths. Breathe in slowly, and out. That is one. [pause 6]
In again, and out. That is two. [pause 5]
Once more, in, and out. That is three. [pause 6]
Now think back over today, or yesterday if today has only just begun. [pause 4]
You are looking for small, simple good things. They do not need to be big. [pause 4]
A warm cup of chai or coffee. A kind word. A seat on the bus. Sunshine through a window. [pause 5]
Let the first good thing come to mind. [pause 8]
Now a second. It can be tiny. [pause 8]
And a third. [pause 8]
If three is hard to find, one is enough. Or simply the fact that you are here, breathing. [pause 4]
Now choose the one that feels warmest, and stay with it for a few breaths. [pause 5]
Picture it clearly. Where were you? Who was there? What could you see, or hear, or feel? [pause 8]
Breathe in, and let the memory be clear. [pause 5]
Breathe out, and let yourself enjoy it. [pause 7]
Notice what happens in your body. Maybe a little warmth, or a softer face. [pause 8]
If a worry shows up, that is okay. Come back to the good thing when you can. [pause 8]
You can say a quiet thank you, in whatever way feels natural. [pause 6]
Now let's carry it forward. Take this feeling with you into the rest of your day. [pause 5]
Take a slow breath in. And out. [pause 5]
When you are ready, open your eyes, and carry on with your day. [pause 4]

## Session — Kindness practice
```json session
{"id": "loving_kindness", "title": "Kindness practice", "minutes": 5, "category": "CALM",
 "description": "A gentle, secular practice of kind wishes: for yourself, someone close, someone neutral, and everyone.", "bestFor": "Softening self-criticism and feeling more connected",
 "ambient": "ocean"}
```
Hello, and welcome. This is a quiet time for kindness. [pause 4]
Sit or lie in whatever way feels comfortable. Close your eyes, or lower your gaze. [pause 4]
Take a slow breath in, and let it go. [pause 5]
In this practice, we offer kind wishes, silently, in simple phrases. You can use mine, or your own. [pause 3]
You can skip any step if it feels hard. If wishing others well feels like too much today, stay with yourself the whole time. That is a good practice too. [pause 5]
Let's begin with you. Picture yourself as you are right now. [pause 5]
May I be safe. [pause 6]
May I be well. [pause 6]
May I be kind to myself. [pause 6]
May I be at ease. [pause 6]
Notice whatever comes up. Warmth, nothing at all, even resistance. Let it be as it is. [pause 8]
Now bring to mind someone close to you. A friend, a family member, a teacher, or a pet. [pause 7]
See their face, or sense their presence. Offer them the same wishes. [pause 5]
May you be safe. [pause 5]
May you be well. [pause 5]
May you be at ease. [pause 6]
If you would rather skip this step, rest here with your breath. [pause 5]
Now someone neutral. Someone you see sometimes but do not know well. A neighbour, a shopkeeper, someone on your bus or train. [pause 7]
They, too, want to feel safe and happy. [pause 4]
May you be safe. [pause 5]
May you be well. [pause 5]
May you be at ease. [pause 6]
Now widen your attention, to everyone. Your street, your town, people far away, in every village and city. [pause 6]
May we all be safe. [pause 6]
May we all be well. [pause 6]
May we all be at ease. [pause 8]
Rest in the stillness for a moment. [pause 10]
Place a hand on your chest or belly, if you like. May I be gentle with myself today. [pause 8]
Take a slow breath in, and out. [pause 5]
When you are ready, open your eyes, and take a breath. [pause 4]
