# Guided Sessions — Calm and Morning

Five short guided sessions read aloud by Android text-to-speech at a calm pace: a morning intention, a stress reset, a grounding practice, a gratitude pause and a kindness practice. Format: DESIGN_PHASE2.md section 3. Each script line is one spoken line, and a trailing pause adds silence after it.

## Session — Morning intention
```json session
{"id": "morning_intention", "title": "Morning intention", "minutes": 3, "category": "MORNING",
 "description": "Breathe, notice how you feel, and choose one simple intention for the day.", "bestFor": "Starting the day with a clear, calm focus",
 "ambient": "focus_pad"}
```
Good morning. [pause 2]
This is a relaxation practice, not a treatment, and you can pause or stop whenever you like. If you feel unwell or very distressed, please reach out to a doctor or another professional. [pause 4]
Find a position that feels comfortable. Sit, stand, or lie down. If you stand, keep your eyes open and stay steady. Otherwise, eyes open or closed. [pause 3]
Take a slow breath in, and let it go. [pause 5]
Once more. In, and out. [pause 4]
Now notice how you feel this morning. Rested, heavy, or somewhere in between. [pause 7]
Whatever is here is welcome. [pause 3]
Let your shoulders drop. [pause 3]
Now think about the day ahead. Not the whole list, just its shape. [pause 4]
Ask yourself, how would I like to meet this day? [pause 5]
Perhaps with patience. Perhaps with energy, kindness, or focus. [pause 3]
Let one word or phrase come to you. Something simple and true. [pause 10]
If no word comes, that is fine. Resting here is enough. [pause 3]
This is your intention for today. Say it quietly to yourself. [pause 6]
Picture, or simply think of, one moment today when it might help. A conversation, a task, or a walk. [pause 7]
You can return to it whenever you like. It is a guide, not a test. [pause 4]
Take one more slow breath in. [pause 3]
And out. [pause 3]
Feel the surface beneath you, and the sounds around you. [pause 3]
When you are ready, open your eyes if they were closed. [pause 3]
Take a breath, and step into your day. [pause 4]

## Session — Two-minute stress reset
```json session
{"id": "stress_reset", "title": "Two-minute stress reset", "minutes": 3, "category": "CALM",
 "description": "Soften your body, take a few long exhales, name the feeling, and choose one small next step.", "bestFor": "A tense moment at work, at home, or on a break",
 "ambient": "pink"}
```
Pause here for a moment, if it is safe to stop where you are. You can reset. [pause 3]
If you are driving or cycling, please save this for later. [pause 2]
Stay seated, or stand and lean on a wall. Seated, eyes open or closed. Standing or out and about, keep them open. [pause 3]
First, let your shoulders drop, away from your ears. [pause 3]
Let your hands soften. Let your jaw loosen, and your tongue rest. [pause 4]
Breathe only as deeply as feels comfortable. If you feel light-headed, breathe normally. [pause 2]
Now a long breath in, through your nose if that suits you. [pause 3]
And a slow, long breath out, as if you are cooling a hot cup of tea. [pause 6]
Again. A long breath in. [pause 3]
And out, a little longer. [pause 5]
One more. Breathe in. [pause 3]
And a long, slow breath out. [pause 5]
Let your breathing return to its own pace. [pause 3]
Notice what you are feeling. Tense muscles, a busy mind, or a restless body. [pause 4]
Give it a name. Tense, rushed, worried, or whatever fits. [pause 3]
You might say quietly to yourself, this is stress, right now. Or use whatever name is true for you. [pause 4]
You are not doing anything wrong. Stress is part of being human. [pause 4]
Now ask yourself, what is one small thing that would help right now? [pause 4]
Maybe sending one message, drinking some water, or stretching, seated or standing. [pause 4]
Choose one, and let it be small enough to feel easy. [pause 4]
Take one more breath, and feel your feet or your seat beneath you. [pause 3]
When you are ready, open your eyes if they were closed, and take your small next step. [pause 3]

## Session — 5-4-3-2-1 grounding
```json session
{"id": "grounding_54321", "title": "5-4-3-2-1 grounding", "minutes": 3, "category": "CALM",
 "description": "Use your senses to come back to the present: five things you see, four you feel, three you hear, two you smell, one you taste.", "bestFor": "When your mind feels busy, scattered or overwhelmed",
 "ambient": null}
```
Begin by noticing that you are here. [pause 3]
Sit, stand, or lie down. Keep your eyes open or closed, whichever feels better. If you stand, keep them open and stay steady. [pause 3]
Please do not use this while driving or cycling. You can stop at any time and look at something neutral. Skip any step that does not feel right today. [pause 2]
Use your senses, counting down from five to one. [pause 2]
Let your next breath be a little slower. [pause 3]
Five things you can see. Look around slowly, and quietly name them. If your eyes are closed, or you cannot see, notice five sounds, or five places your body touches something. [pause 12]
Four things you can feel. The weight of your body, and your clothes on your skin. [pause 5]
The air on your face. Your hands, warm or cool. [pause 6]
Three things you can hear. Near, then far. A fan, traffic, birds, or the quiet itself. If hearing is difficult, notice three things you can feel or see instead. [pause 10]
Two things you can smell, if you can. Perhaps tea, soap, cooking, or fresh air. If not, imagine two smells you love, or take two slow breaths. [pause 8]
One thing you can taste. Notice any taste in your mouth. If you have water nearby and it suits you, take a sip. Or take one slow, full breath. [pause 8]
Now notice how you feel, compared with a few minutes ago. [pause 5]
Nothing needs to have changed. Noticing is enough. [pause 3]
Whenever things feel fast, you can pause and use your senses like this. [pause 3]
When you are ready, take a breath, and open your eyes if they were closed. [pause 4]

## Session — Gratitude pause
```json session
{"id": "gratitude", "title": "Gratitude pause", "minutes": 4, "category": "CALM",
 "description": "Three breaths, three simple good things from your day, and one to savour and carry forward.", "bestFor": "Ending a busy day, or any time you want to notice the good",
 "ambient": "rain"}
```
Welcome. Take a few minutes to notice what is good. [pause 4]
Settle into a position that feels easy. Keep your eyes open, resting on something calm, or let them close. [pause 4]
Begin with three breaths. Breathe in slowly, and out. [pause 6]
Again. In, and out. [pause 5]
Once more, in, and out. [pause 6]
Now think back over today, or the last day or two. [pause 4]
You are looking for small, simple good things. They do not need to be big. If three is hard to find, one is enough, and if none comes today, that is okay too. [pause 4]
A warm cup of chai or coffee. A kind word. A seat on the bus. Sunshine through a window. [pause 5]
Let the first good thing come to mind. [pause 8]
Now a second. It can be tiny. [pause 8]
And a third. [pause 8]
If nothing comes, rest with your breath. Simply being here, breathing, is enough. [pause 4]
Now choose the one that feels warmest, and stay with it for a few breaths. [pause 5]
Bring it to mind. Where were you? Who was there? What could you see, or hear, or feel? [pause 8]
Breathe in, and let the memory be clear. [pause 5]
Breathe out, and notice what that is like. [pause 7]
Notice what happens in your body. Maybe a little warmth, or a softer face. [pause 8]
If worry, sadness or a difficult memory shows up, that is okay. You can choose a different good thing, look around the room, or rest with your breath. [pause 8]
You can say a quiet thank you, in whatever way feels natural. [pause 6]
Take whatever you notice with you, even if it is just a little calm. [pause 5]
Take one last slow breath. [pause 5]
When you are ready, open your eyes if they were closed, and carry on with whatever comes next. [pause 4]

## Session — Kindness practice
```json session
{"id": "loving_kindness", "title": "Kindness practice", "minutes": 5, "category": "CALM",
 "description": "A gentle, secular practice of kind wishes: for yourself, someone close, someone neutral, and everyone.", "bestFor": "A quiet moment of kind wishes for yourself and others",
 "ambient": "ocean"}
```
Hello. This is a quiet time for kindness. [pause 4]
Sit or lie in whatever way feels comfortable. Close your eyes, lower your gaze, or keep them open, whichever feels safest. [pause 4]
Let one slow breath come, and go. [pause 5]
In this practice, I will say a few simple phrases. Repeat them silently in your mind, or use your own words. [pause 3]
You can skip any step if it feels hard. [pause 2]
If wishing others well feels like too much today, stay with the wishes for yourself. That is a good practice too. [pause 3]
If wishing yourself well is the hard part, you can imagine someone who cares about you offering these wishes, or simply rest with your breath. [pause 4]
Begin with you. Sense yourself as you are right now, or just rest on your breath. [pause 5]
May I be safe. [pause 6]
May I be well. [pause 6]
May I be kind to myself. [pause 6]
May I be at ease. [pause 6]
Notice whatever comes up. Warmth, nothing at all, even resistance. Let it be as it is. [pause 8]
Now bring to mind someone it is easy to care about. A friend, a family member, a teacher, or a pet. If that feels hard today, rest with your breath instead. [pause 7]
See their face, or sense their presence. Offer them the same wishes. [pause 5]
May you be safe. [pause 5]
May you be well. [pause 5]
May you be at ease. [pause 6]
Now someone neutral. Someone you see sometimes but do not know well. A neighbour, a shopkeeper, someone on your bus or train. [pause 7]
They want to feel safe, just like you. [pause 4]
May you be safe. [pause 5]
May you be well. [pause 5]
May you be at ease. [pause 6]
Now widen your attention, to everyone. Your street, your town, people far away, in every village and city. [pause 6]
May we all be safe. [pause 6]
May we all be well. [pause 6]
May we all be at ease. [pause 8]
Rest in the stillness for a moment. [pause 10]
Place a hand on your chest or belly, if you like. [pause 4]
May I be gentle with myself today. [pause 8]
Take a slow breath in, and out. Notice the surface beneath you, and the sounds in the room. [pause 5]
When you are ready, let your eyes open if they were closed. [pause 4]
