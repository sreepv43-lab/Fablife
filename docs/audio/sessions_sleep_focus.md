# Guided Sessions — Sleep, Focus and Mindful Eating

Five guided sessions read aloud by Android text-to-speech at a calm pace. Format: DESIGN_PHASE2.md section 3. Each script line is one spoken line, and a trailing pause adds silence after it.

## Session — Mindful eating

If you have a complicated relationship with food, go gently, and skip anything that doesn't feel right. [pause 5]

Notice the colours, the shapes, and the steam if there is any. [pause 5]

Bring the food a little closer, and breathe in. [pause 5]

Check in with your body, if that feels helpful. Are you hungry, or not so much? Either answer is fine. [pause 5]

Swallow when you're ready. [pause 3]
