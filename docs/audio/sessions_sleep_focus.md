# Guided Sessions — Sleep, Focus and Mindful Eating

Five guided sessions read aloud by Android text-to-speech at a calm pace. Format: DESIGN_PHASE2.md section 3. Each script line is one spoken line, and a trailing pause adds silence after it.

## Session — Mindful eating
```json session

 "ambient": null}
```

This works with a full meal, or just a small snack. Any food at all. [pause 3]

However you eat, and whatever you eat, is welcome here. Soft or simple foods are just as welcome. [pause 3]
Take a slow breath in, and a slow breath out. [pause 5]
If you have a complicated relationship with food, go gently, and skip anything that does not feel right. [pause 5]
There are no good or bad foods in this practice. There is only you, and what is in front of you. [pause 5]
Begin by looking. [pause 3]
Notice the colours, the shapes, and the steam if there is any. [pause 6]
Now notice the smell. [pause 3]
Bring it a little closer, and breathe in. [pause 6]
Check in with your body. Are you hungry, or not so much? Either answer is fine. [pause 6]
Now take your first bite. Make it a small one. [pause 4]
Before you chew, notice the food resting in your mouth. [pause 4]
Chew slowly. What flavours do you notice? Sweet, salty, sour, spicy, or something else? [pause 6]
What about the texture? Soft, crunchy, creamy, or smooth? [pause 8]
Swallow when you are ready. [pause 4]
Now put down your spoon, or your hands, for a moment. [pause 6]
Continue eating at your own pace. [pause 4]
Let each bite be a little slower than usual. [pause 6]
Take your time. I will be quiet while you eat. [pause 12]
[pause 12]
Now pause, around the halfway point. [pause 4]
Put down your fork, spoon or hands. Take a breath. [pause 4]
How does your body feel now? Are you still hungry, or comfortably satisfied? [pause 6]
Whatever you notice is simply information. There is nothing to judge. [pause 5]
Carry on, if you wish, or stop if you are done. Both are fine. [pause 5]
Take a moment to thank the food, and the people who grew it, cooked it or shared it with you. [pause 5]
And be kind to your body. It is doing its best for you. [pause 4]
Take a breath, and bring your attention back to the room. [pause 4]
When you're ready, carry on with your meal, or with your day. [pause 3]
