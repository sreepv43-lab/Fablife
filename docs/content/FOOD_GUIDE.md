# Fab Life — Healthy Food Recommendations

Source content for the in-app **Eat** section (see [PRODUCT_SPEC.md §6.11](../PRODUCT_SPEC.md#611-food-recommendations)). Meal ideas are filtered by the user's **diet type**, **allergies** and the **Gentle on the stomach (low-fibre)** setting.

> **Disclaimer shown in the app:** Fab Life gives general healthy-eating ideas, not medical or dietary treatment. If you have a medical condition (for example diabetes, kidney disease, IBS, IBD, coeliac disease or food allergies), are pregnant, or have ongoing stomach problems, follow the advice of your doctor or a registered dietitian.

---

## 1. Healthy Eating Principles

These principles appear as short cards in the Eat section and are referenced in journey letters.

1. **Build a balanced plate.** About half vegetables and fruit, a quarter protein, and a quarter grains or starchy foods (whole grains when your stomach is comfortable with them), plus a little healthy fat.
2. **Include protein at every meal.** It keeps you full and supports muscles: dal, beans, paneer, tofu, curd/yoghurt, eggs, fish, chicken, nuts and seeds.
3. **Choose mostly whole, minimally processed foods.** Cook at home when you can.
4. **Go easy on added sugar.** Fewer sweets, sugary drinks and packaged snacks. Fruit is the better sweet.
5. **Go easy on salt and deep-fried food.** Flavour with herbs, spices, lemon, garlic and ginger instead.
6. **Use healthy fats in moderation.** Nuts, seeds, avocado, and oils like mustard, groundnut or olive oil, rather than lots of butter, ghee or vanaspati.
7. **Drink water through the day.** Let thirst and pale-yellow urine guide you. Plain water, buttermilk (chaas), unsweetened tea or infused water rather than sugary drinks.
8. **Eat regularly and mindfully.** Sit down, slow down and stop when comfortably full.

## 4. Gentle on the Stomach (low-fibre option)

### 4.1 Who it is for

For users who say high-fibre foods (raw salads, beans, whole grains, skins and seeds) make their stomach uncomfortable, for example bloating, gas, cramping or loose stools. It is also useful for a few days when your stomach is upset or recovering.

**App guidance shown when the setting is turned on:**

> Gentle meals use foods that are easier to digest: well-cooked, peeled and soft vegetables, refined grains like white rice, and lean proteins.
>
> A low-fibre diet is usually meant to be **short-term or guided by your doctor**. Fibre is important for long-term gut and heart health. When you feel better, try adding fibre back slowly, one food at a time, in small amounts.
>
> **See a doctor if** you have stomach pain that doesn't go away, blood in your stool, black stools, unexplained weight loss, vomiting, diarrhoea lasting more than a few days, difficulty swallowing, or symptoms that wake you at night.

### 4.2 Choose more often

| Food group | Gentle choices |
|---|---|
| **Grains** | White rice, idli, plain dosa, rava/sooji upma, white bread or toast, plain pasta, semolina, rice flakes (poha, well-cooked), a small soft phulka if tolerated |
| **Vegetables** | Well-cooked and peeled: bottle gourd (lauki), pumpkin, carrot, potato and sweet potato without skin, ridge gourd, zucchini without seeds, spinach (well-cooked, puréed) |
| **Fruit** | Ripe banana, papaya, peeled and stewed apple or applesauce, melon, peeled pear (stewed), canned fruit in juice |
| **Protein** | Eggs, paneer, tofu, fish, skinless chicken, well-cooked and strained moong dal or dal water, smooth nut butter |
| **Dairy** (if tolerated) | Curd/yoghurt, buttermilk, milk, lactose-free milk |
| **Fats** | Small amounts of oil or ghee for cooking |
| **Drinks** | Water, buttermilk, clear soups, rice kanji, coconut water, weak tea |

### 4.3 Limit while your stomach is sensitive

- Raw vegetables and large salads
- Whole grains and bran (brown rice, large amounts of whole-wheat atta, wheat or oat bran, millets)
- Beans, chickpeas, rajma, whole lentils and sprouts
- Fruit and vegetable skins and seeds, dried fruit, berries with seeds
- Whole nuts and seeds (smooth nut butter is fine)
- Cabbage, cauliflower, broccoli, onion and garlic in large amounts (can cause gas)
- Very spicy, oily or deep-fried food
- Carbonated drinks, and large amounts of coffee or alcohol

### 4.4 Gentle eating habits

- Eat **smaller meals more often** (5–6 small meals instead of 3 large).
- **Cook vegetables until soft**; peel and remove seeds.
- **Chew well** and eat slowly.
- **Drink fluids between meals** rather than lots with meals.
- Keep a short **food and symptom note** for a week to spot your personal triggers (available in the app as an optional checkbox per meal, Phase 2).
- **Reintroduce fibre gradually**: add one food (e.g. oats or a peeled cooked apple) every few days. Soluble-fibre foods like oats, ripe banana and peeled cooked fruit are often easier than raw vegetables or bran.
