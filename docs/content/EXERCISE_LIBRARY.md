# Fab Life — Exercise Recommendations

Source content for the in-app **Move** section (see [PRODUCT_SPEC.md §6.12](../PRODUCT_SPEC.md#612-exercise-recommendations)). Routines are filtered by the user's **fitness level**, **available time** and **movement needs** chosen in onboarding or Settings.

> **Safety note shown in the app:** Start slowly and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual shortness of breath. If you are pregnant, have a heart, lung, joint or other medical condition, are recovering from injury or surgery, or haven't exercised in a long time, check with your doctor before starting a new exercise routine.

---

## 1. How Much Movement? (weekly guidance)

Based on the World Health Organization's physical activity guidelines for adults:

| Type | Recommendation | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Aerobic (cardio)** | 150–300 min/week of moderate activity, **or** 75–150 min/week of vigorous activity | Brisk walking, cycling, dancing, swimming, jogging |
| **Strength** | 2 or more days/week, working all major muscle groups | Squats, push-ups, resistance bands, carrying loads |
| **Balance & flexibility** | Especially important from age 65; helpful for everyone | Yoga, tai chi, standing on one leg, stretching |
| **Less sitting** | Break up long periods of sitting | Stand up and move for 2–3 minutes every hour |

**How to tell the intensity (talk test):**
- **Light:** you can sing.
- **Moderate:** you can talk but not sing.
- **Vigorous:** you can only say a few words before pausing for breath.

**The app message:** *Some movement is always better than none.* Journey 1 starts with 5 minutes a day. The library helps users build up gradually towards the weekly guidance.

## 3. Exercise Glossary (single moves)

Each move has: name, muscles worked, how-to cues (max 3), easier version, harder version. The app shows a simple illustration or looped animation.

| Move | How to do it | Easier | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|
| **March in place** | Lift knees to hip height, swing arms, stand tall | Lower knees, hold a chair | High knees, faster pace |
| **Squat** | Feet hip-width, sit hips back as if onto a chair, knees follow toes, chest up | Sit-to-stand from a chair | Slow 3-second lowering, or jump squat |
| **Wall push-up** | Hands on wall at shoulder height, body straight, bend elbows to bring chest to wall | Stand closer to the wall | Incline push-up on a table, then floor push-up |
| **Glute bridge** | Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat; squeeze glutes and lift hips; lower slowly | Smaller lift | Single-leg bridge |
| **Bird-dog** | On hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg, keep back flat, switch | Extend only arm or only leg | Hold 5 seconds each side |
| **Dead bug** | On back, arms up, knees bent at 90°; lower opposite arm and leg, keep lower back on floor | Move only legs | Slower, fully extend |
| **Plank** | Forearms on floor, body in a straight line from head to heels, breathe | Knees down, or plank on a table | Longer hold, lift one foot |
| **Reverse lunge** | Step one foot back, lower both knees toward 90°, push back up | Hold a chair, smaller step | Alternate without pausing |
| **Calf raise** | Rise onto the balls of your feet, lower slowly | Hold a wall | Single leg |
| **Arm circles** | Arms out to the side, small circles forward then backward | Smaller circles | Bigger, faster circles |
| **Cat–cow** | On hands and knees, round your back (cat), then gently arch (cow), with breath | Seated version | Slower, deeper breath |
| **Child's pose** | Kneel, sit back on heels, reach arms forward, rest forehead down | Pillow under hips/forehead | Walk hands to each side |
| **Standing side bend** | Reach one arm overhead and lean gently to the opposite side | Hand on hip | Hold 20 seconds |
| **Hamstring stretch** | Heel on a low step, leg straight, hinge forward from hips | Seated, reach toward shin | Hold 30 seconds |
| **Seated march** | Sit tall at chair edge, lift knees alternately, swing arms | Arms only | Faster pace |
| **Seated leg extension** | Sit tall, straighten one knee, hold 2 s, lower | Smaller range | Add ankle weight |
| **Seated torso twist** | Sit tall, hand on opposite knee, rotate gently | Smaller rotation | Hold 15 seconds |
| **Single-leg balance** | Stand on one foot near a wall or chair | Fingertips on the chair | Eyes closed, near support |

## 4. Routines

Format: each routine is a list of `move — time or reps`. The ritual player shows one move per screen with a timer, then a 10–15 second rest unless stated.

### 4.1 Wake-Up Routines (used by Journey 1, Step 4)

**R1. Gentle Wake-Up — 5 min · Beginner · Standard**
1. March in place — 60 s
2. Arm circles — 30 s (15 forward, 15 back)
3. Standing side bend — 30 s (15 each side)
4. Squat (or sit-to-stand) — 10 reps
5. Wall push-up — 10 reps
6. Calf raise — 15 reps
7. Deep breaths with arms reaching up — 5 breaths

**R2. Energy Boost — 5 min · Intermediate · Standard**
1. High-knee march — 45 s
2. Squat — 15 reps
3. Incline push-up (table/counter) — 10 reps
4. Reverse lunge — 10 reps (5 each leg)
5. Plank — 30 s
6. Jumping jacks or step jacks (low-impact) — 45 s
7. Stretch: reach up, fold forward — 30 s

**R3. Joint-Friendly Wake-Up — 5 min · Any level · Low-impact**
1. March in place, holding a chair if needed — 60 s
2. Shoulder rolls — 30 s
3. Sit-to-stand from a chair — 8 reps
4. Wall push-up — 8 reps
5. Calf raise holding a chair — 10 reps
6. Cat–cow (standing, hands on thighs) — 30 s
7. Slow breathing — 5 breaths

**R4. Seated Wake-Up — 5 min · Seated / limited mobility**
1. Seated march — 60 s
2. Arm circles — 30 s
3. Seated leg extension — 10 each leg
4. Seated torso twist — 30 s
5. Punches forward, alternating — 30 s
6. Neck: slow look left and right — 30 s
7. Deep breaths with arms up — 5 breaths

**R5. Sun Salutation Flow — 5 min · Beginner–Intermediate · Standard**
Slow Surya Namaskar, 3–5 rounds, one breath per movement. Beginner version: keep knees on the floor in plank, and use cobra instead of upward dog.

**R6. Morning Walk — 5–10 min · Any level · Outdoors**
A brisk walk where you can talk but not sing. Counts for both *Move* and *Morning light* (Journey 1, Step 5).

### 4.2 Strength Routines (2–3×/week)

**S1. Beginner Full Body — 10 min · No equipment**
Two rounds of:
1. Sit-to-stand — 10 reps
2. Wall push-up — 10 reps
3. Glute bridge — 12 reps
4. Bird-dog — 6 each side
5. Calf raise — 15 reps
6. Rest — 60 s between rounds

**S2. Intermediate Full Body — 20 min**
Three rounds of:
1. Squat — 15 reps
2. Push-up (knees or full) — 8–12 reps
3. Reverse lunge — 10 each leg
4. Glute bridge — 15 reps
5. Plank — 30–45 s
6. Dead bug — 8 each side
7. Rest — 60–90 s between rounds

**S3. Active Full Body — 30 min**
Four rounds of: jump squat or tempo squat ×15, push-up ×15, walking lunge ×20, single-leg bridge ×10 each side, plank 60 s, mountain climbers 30 s. Rest 60 s between rounds.

**S4. Seated Strength — 10 min · Seated / limited mobility**
Two rounds of: seated leg extension ×10 each leg, seated march 45 s, punches 30 s, overhead press with water bottles ×10, seated torso twist 30 s, sit-to-stand (if able) ×5.

### 4.3 Cardio

| Routine | Time | Level | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C1. Brisk walk** | 10–30 min | Any | Low |
| **C2. Dance break**: 3–6 songs you love | 10–20 min | Any | Low–Standard |
| **C3. Indoor low-impact cardio**: march, step-touch, knee lifts, arm punches, side steps, 40 s each with 20 s rest ×3 | 10 min | Beginner | Low |
| **C4. Interval walk**: 2 min easy + 1 min fast ×6 | 18 min | Intermediate | Low |
| **C5. Cycling or swimming** | 20–30 min | Any | Low |
| **C6. Jog/walk intervals**: 1 min jog + 2 min walk ×8 | 24 min | Intermediate | Standard |

### 4.4 Flexibility & Wind-Down (Evening ritual, future journeys)

**F1. Desk Reset — 5 min**: neck rolls, shoulder rolls, chest opener (hands clasped behind), seated twist, hamstring stretch, 30 s each, repeat.

**F2. Evening Stretch — 10 min**: cat–cow 60 s, child's pose 60 s, hamstring stretch 30 s each, figure-four hip stretch 30 s each, lying twist 30 s each, legs up the wall 2 min, slow breathing 1 min.

**F3. Balance Builder — 5 min**: single-leg balance 30 s each (×2), heel-to-toe walk 60 s, calf raise 15, side leg lift holding a chair 10 each.

Every plan follows one rule: **increase total time by no more than about 10% per week.**
